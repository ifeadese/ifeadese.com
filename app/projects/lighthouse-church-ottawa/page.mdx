# Lighthouse Church Ottawa

I attend Lighthouse Church in Ottawa and serve on a team with a unique role of managing our church website. Recently we did a rebuild. A different design, a different site, a different host, everything. I started contributing after the foundational design elements were in place, and I had some creative freedom as well. Completed within a month. The goal was to deliver a site that looks "inviting" while creatively reflecting the church's story, values, community and what we actually do.

## What Was Delivered

A Next.js app deployed on Vercel with a light-themed UI, rich in images and designed following the pre-existing Lighthouse brand guide while adopting Claude Design's typical combination of italics and an accent color (magenta) for emphasis in text.

- **30+ pages** in total, covering four campus congregations, the Academy, events, and every next step someone can take after a Sunday.
- **About pages.** Who We Are, Our Beliefs, Our Team, Locations, and a Plan Your Visit written separately for each congregation, so a first time visitor reads the answers for the room they are actually walking into.
- **Event pages.** An events index, a page per event, and full landings for the church's flagship events. A major event's landing runs on its own colour palette and typefaces, so it looks like itself without ever leaving the site.
- **Academy pages.** Three tracks, Core, Discipleship and Marriage, holding nine classes. Each class gets its own page, sign up path and FAQ.
- **Contact pages.** Contact, Connect, Give, Next Steps, and Pastoral Care with direct booking into the care team's calendars. Every public form, from the connect card to prayer requests to baptism sign ups, writes through one shared intake path.
- **SEO controls** on every page type. Meta tags, OG images and canonical URLs built from a single helper, structured data for the church, its campuses and its events, a sitemap generated from real content rather than a hand kept list, and 28 redirects carrying the old Squarespace URLs to their new homes so nothing that was already ranking went dark.
