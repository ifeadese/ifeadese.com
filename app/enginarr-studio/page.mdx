# Enginarr Studio

Enginarr Studio is my web design and development practice. I make websites for individuals and small businesses — sites that are neat, fast, mobile-responsive and user-friendly.

It's a studio of one, which is the point. You work directly with the person designing and building your site, so there's no handoff between a designer, a developer and an account manager, and nothing gets lost in between.

## Who It's For

People with something specific to put online and no interest in wrestling with a site builder to do it — a product, a service, an event, a portfolio, a small business that needs to look credible when someone searches for it.

## How It Works

- **Initial Chat**: ~15 mins. We'll talk about your project, timeline etc
- **Plan**: Research and design according to our discussion, then get your feedback
- **Build**: Proceed to build what we've agreed on
- **Handover**: Necessary deliverables given to you
- **Launch**: Make it live and share

Hosting, branding and SEO are handled as needed along the way.

## Packages

- ***Simple***: For sites with a few pages and common conventional features. If needed, this can include a basic logo and other branding elements for free.

    Starting at $300. Delivered within 5-7 days. Ideal for individuals or small businesses needing marketing-focused landing pages for products, services, events, portfolio etc.

- ***Standard***: For small to mid-sized sites, cohesively-branded across 5+ pages, and will require custom features e.g. forms, third-party integrations, etc.

    Starting at $1000. Delivered within 1-2 weeks. Ideal for projects with uncommon requirements and some level of complexity.

Every project is quoted after the initial chat, once the scope is clear.
